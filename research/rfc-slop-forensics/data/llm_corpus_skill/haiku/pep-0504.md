## Abstract

The module-level functions of the `random` module use the Mersenne Twister, a deterministic pseudorandom generator. A developer who does security-sensitive work must know to switch to `os.urandom`, to `random.SystemRandom`, or to a third-party library such as `cryptography`. Developers who do not know their work is security-sensitive keep the defaults, and their users carry the risk.

The abstract then says that this is a chronic problem rather than an acute one, and that the delay between introducing a security flaw and its exploitation makes the lesson hard to learn from experience. The abstract is cut off before the proposal begins. The title states the proposal: the module-level APIs would use the system random number generator by default.
