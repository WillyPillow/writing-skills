## Abstract

This PEP combines two earlier proposals. From PEP 310 it takes the reliable acquisition and release pairs. From PEP 340 it takes the anonymous block statements. The PEP aims to keep the parts of PEP 340 that are useful, blend them with parts of PEP 310, and rearrange the result. It borrows from several other PEPs and is intended to stand on its own.
