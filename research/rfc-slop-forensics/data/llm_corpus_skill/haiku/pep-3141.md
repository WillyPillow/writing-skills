## Abstract

This PEP defines a hierarchy of abstract base classes (ABCs), using the mechanism of PEP 3119, to represent number-like classes. The hierarchy is written `Number :> Complex :> Real :> Rational :> Integral`, where `A :> B` means that A is a supertype of B. The ordering is inspired by the numeric tower of Scheme. ## The hierarchy

The notation reads from broad to narrow. Following the abstract's definition of `A :> B`, Complex is a supertype of Real, so every Real is also a Complex. Likewise every Rational is a Real, every Integral is a Rational, and every class in the chain is a Number. The chain has five classes and four supertype steps.
