## Summary

This RFC proposes the 2017 Rust Roadmap, in accordance with RFC 1728. The goal of the roadmap is to lay out a vision for where the Rust project should be in a year's time. The focus for this year is improving Rust's productivity while keeping its emphasis on fast, reliable code.

By the end of 2017, the material says, Rust should have the following properties:

* a lower learning curve;
* a pleasant edit-compile-debug cycle;
* a solid, but basic, IDE experience;
* easy access to high-quality crates;
* a good fit for writing robust, high-scale servers;
* 1.0-level crates for essential tasks;
* easy integration into large build systems;
* community mentoring at all levels. [TODO: The material ends in the middle of this item, at "community mentoring at all". Complete the item from the full text.]
